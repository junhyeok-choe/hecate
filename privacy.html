Privacy policy

This policy covers Hecate's website and its personal Google Drive connection through rclone.

Google Drive access

With authorization, rclone can view, download, upload, rename, move, and delete Drive files, depending on the permissions granted. The full Drive scope covers all files accessible to the account. A folder setting in rclone limits its working view, not its Google permissions.

Use and storage

The connection is used to mount and manage Google Drive files at the account holder's request. rclone stores OAuth tokens in its local configuration and may cache files on the device. File operations communicate with Google Drive. This website does not receive Drive files or OAuth credentials.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no contact forms or analytics scripts. GitHub may process standard website access information under its privacy statement.

Revoking access

Google Drive access can be revoked in Google Account settings. Locally stored tokens and cached files can be removed separately; revoking access does not delete local copies.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the Hecate GitHub repository.
