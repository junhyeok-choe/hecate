Personal application integration

A defined boundary for personal integrations.

Hecate documents the boundaries and data-access practices of independently operated integrations. Its current use case is a personal rclone connection to Google Drive.

Informational site only. This website does not authenticate with Google, proxy Google Drive requests, receive OAuth tokens, or handle Drive files. Authorization occurs between the local rclone client and Google.

Current integration: Google Drive via rclone

Purpose

Mount and manage files in a Google Drive account from an operator-controlled device. The client may list, read, upload, rename, move, and delete files as explicitly requested by the operator.

Access

The Google Drive full-access OAuth scope can authorize broad access to accessible Drive files. A configured rclone root folder narrows its working view; it is not an OAuth permission boundary.

Data handling

OAuth credentials and caches are handled by the local rclone installation. No Drive content or OAuth credentials are submitted to this GitHub Pages site.

Scope and status

This is a personal-use integration, not a hosted cloud-storage product or a public OAuth service. Future connectors, agents, or MCP integrations are not represented as active features here.

Review the privacy policy for the current data-access boundaries, and consult rclone's official OAuth setup instructions for configuration.
